The predictions are a model

Win probabilities, projected alliance selections, bracket odds and event difficulty ratings are produced by a statistical model fitted to past match scores. The model does not know about a broken drivetrain, a driver having a good day, a strategy nobody has run yet, or anything that has not already happened on a field.

They will sometimes be wrong, including confidently wrong. Treat them as one more input, the way you would treat any other scout's opinion. Do not make a decision you cannot afford to get wrong — an alliance pick, a bracket strategy, which event to register for — on the strength of a number on this site alone.
