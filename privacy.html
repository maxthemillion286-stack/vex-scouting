Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

VEX Scout has no accounts, no sign-up and no user database. Everything you create in it — saved teams, your theme, Jumper anchors, events saved for offline use — is stored by your own browser and never sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

The app uses your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB. Nothing in either leaves your device. Specifically:

  • Your chosen colour theme and the tab you had open last.
  • Team numbers you have scouted or compared.
  • Whether the prediction switch is on, and the season list the app caches so it does not refetch it every visit.
  • Jumper video anchors and calibrations, and which matches you have already watched.
  • Events you have saved for offline use, including their match and ranking data.

Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, which also means there is nothing for us to hand back if you lose it.

What the server sees

VEX Scout is a static page. It talks to one endpoint of its own, /api/proxy, which forwards requests to the RobotEvents API and the YouTube Data API. It does that server-side so the API keys stay on the server and never reach your browser.

Those requests carry what you asked for — a team number, an event ID, a season — and not who you are. No identifier is attached to them and none is stored. The proxy briefly caches the answers it gets back, keyed by what was asked rather than by who asked, so that two people looking up the same event do not each cost a fresh API call.

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard web server logs (IP address, time, requested path, browser user-agent) for operating and securing the service. That is Vercel's processing, described in their privacy policy.

No tracking

There is no analytics on this site. No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting. VEX Scout sets no cookies of its own — the app never touches document.cookie at all.

Third parties

Some parts of the page are loaded from other companies. When your browser fetches from them, it tells them your IP address and browser details, as it must to receive a reply. Their own policies govern what they do with that.

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — the three typefaces the app uses. This happens on every page load. Google's privacy policy.
  • YouTube — VEX Scout uses the YouTube Data API to find match videos, and embeds the YouTube player to show them. By using the video features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's privacy policy applies to data Google collects. The embedded player may store data and set cookies in your browser. You can review and revoke the data Google holds about you at Google's security settings page.
  • Vimeo and BoxCast — some events stream on these instead. Their embedded players behave the same way, under Vimeo's and BoxCast's policies.
  • RobotEvents — where all team, match and skills data comes from. The app reads it through the proxy above; nothing about you is sent to it.

The video embeds only load once you actually open a video. Until then, none of YouTube, Vimeo or BoxCast is contacted.

The app also builds ordinary links out to robotevents.com, events.vex.com and vexworlds.tv so you can open an event's official page or a live stream. Following one is a normal navigation to someone else's website, and from that point their policies apply, not this one.

Young users

VEX competitors are often under 13, so this matters: VEX Scout does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. There is no account to create, no form to fill in, and no field anywhere that asks for a name, an email address or a location. Please don't type personal information into the site — there is no reason to, and nothing is built to handle it. The third-party embeds listed above are the one place data about a visitor can reach another company, which is why they are listed.
